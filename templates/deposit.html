Deposit Money

Balance update and transaction entry are committed together

What happens on the server?

  1. The account row is selected with FOR UPDATE inside a MySQL transaction.
  2. The account must exist and be Active.
  3. The balance is increased and a SUCCESS deposit row is inserted.
  4. Both changes are committed; any failure triggers a rollback.
