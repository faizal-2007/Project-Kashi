Transfer Money

Two balance updates and two transaction rows in one atomic MySQL transaction

Transfer rules

  • Source and destination must be different Active accounts.
  • Rows are locked in AccountID order to reduce deadlock risk.
  • Both balances and both transaction records commit or roll back together.
  • TRANSFER_OUT and TRANSFER_IN share a common TransferGroupReference.
